Tipio

Know what you really make.

Tipio logs your tips and hours in seconds and shows your real hourly pay, your best shifts and your totals for tax time. For servers, bartenders, baristas, hosts, bussers, salon and valet staff.

No account needed. Nothing to lose. No sign-up and no server that can lose years of tips. Your shifts stay on your iPhone and in your iPhone backups, and you can save a backup or a spreadsheet anytime, free. Turn on automatic backup (one tap when you start) and a dated copy lands in a folder you pick, like iCloud Drive, every day you log. On a new iPhone, choose the same folder and Tipio offers to restore it.
Log a shift in seconds. Card tips, cash tips and tip-out, with times or just hours. Your usual tip-out is one tap away, and a reminder comes at a time you pick on the days you work, only if you haven't logged yet. Your real hourly pay counts the tips you kept, your base pay and automatic gratuities.
Your month at a glance. A calendar of what each day paid, a month-end forecast from your own last 90 days, and a pay stub check for your pay period, weekly, every two weeks or twice a month. All free.
Tax time, sorted. Monthly tip totals for each employer, due by the 10th, and the tips you kept this year for the federal tip deduction (2025–2028). For personal record-keeping only: estimates from your own entries, not tax advice. Tipio is not affiliated with or endorsed by the IRS.
Free for 2 jobs. A main job and a side gig, with unlimited shifts. Tipio Pro adds insights (best days, hourly pay by week), forecast what-ifs, a tax-year tip report for your preparer, a third job and more, and goals: a weekly goal on the Shifts tab and a monthly goal checked inside the forecast. Never charged per job. The free version shows one small ad at the end of your shifts list, never over your numbers; Pro removes it. Try the annual plan free for 7 days, with a reminder before it ends if notifications are on, or pay once.
