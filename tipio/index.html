What it does

Your real hourly pay

Log card tips, cash tips and tip-out in seconds. Tipio counts the tips you kept, your base pay and automatic gratuities, divided by your hours.

A timer for the shift you’re on

Start it from New shift. It runs on your Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island and above the tab bar; end it to log the tips with the times filled in.

Your month at a glance

A calendar of what each day paid, a month-end forecast from your own last 90 days, and a pay stub check for your pay period.

Tax time, sorted

Monthly tip totals for each employer, due by the 10th, and the tips you kept this year for the federal tip deduction (2025–2028). For personal record-keeping only, not tax advice.

Your data stays yours

Your shifts stay on your iPhone and never go to a server, even if you sign in (sign-in is optional). Free daily backup to a folder you pick; Pro adds iCloud backup that restores itself on a new iPhone.
