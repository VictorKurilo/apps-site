No Tax on Tips? Keep the record that backs it up.

From 2025 to 2028, tipped workers can deduct up to $25,000 of qualified tips from federal income tax. Tipio keeps the tips you kept apart from automatic gratuities, and adds them up for the year.

  • Auto-gratuities and service charges kept apart, since they don’t qualify
  • Each employer’s monthly tip report, with a reminder before the 10th

FAQ

Questions servers ask

Anything else? Support answers within two working days.

Is Tipio free?

Yes. Logging, the shift timer, 2 jobs, the calendar, the month-end forecast, the pay stub check and the monthly tip report are free, with one small ad. Pro is $12.99 a year (the first 7 days free), $1.99 a month or $29.99 once.

Does Tipio do my taxes?

No. It keeps a record of what you enter and adds it up. It doesn’t file anything or give tax advice; your W-2 and a tax professional have the final word.

Do my tips count for No Tax on Tips?

Tips customers choose to give, in a job on the IRS list of tipped occupations, usually do: up to $25,000 per return for 2025–2028, less above $150,000 of income ($300,000 joint). Automatic gratuities and service charges don’t. Check IRS.gov for your case.

Where are my tips stored?

On your iPhone, in your normal iPhone and iCloud backups, in any folder you pick for the daily copy and, with Pro, in your own private iCloud. Signing in is optional, and your shifts never go to a server either way.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes. Export a CSV from your old tip app or spreadsheet and import it in Settings. You see a preview first, and duplicates are skipped.

I deliver or do gig work. Does it fit?

You can log tips and other pay (fares, fees, commission) for each job. Mileage isn’t tracked.
